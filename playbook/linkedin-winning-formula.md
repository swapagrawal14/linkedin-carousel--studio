# My LinkedIn Winning Formula (from my own data, Oct 2026)

## What hit (Jan to Mar 2026)
| Post | Format | Reactions | Comments |
|---|---|---|---|
| BSSO | PDF carousel | 320 | 8 |
| Dental Indices | PDF carousel | 264 | 27 |
| Nerve Injury | PDF carousel | 168 | 14 |
| All-on-4 implant talks | Short animation | 167 | 11 |
| Endo Surgery | PDF carousel | 102 | 6 |

## What flopped (Mar to Oct 2026)
Long 2 to 3 min videos, AI/app/ebook posts and posts with links: 1 to 31 reactions. There was also a 4-month gap (Mar 8 to Jul 31) with only 1 post.

## The recipe
1. **Fear hook** aimed at "you" (under 10 words)
2. **Textbook vs reality** angle
3. **Right crowd**: dental students, PG aspirants, residents
4. **PDF carousel**, 8 to 12 slides
5. **Either/or question** at the end ("OHI-S or CPITN?" = 27 comments)
6. **Save · Share · Tag** line
7. **Zero links** in the post (put them in the first comment)
8. 3 to 5 hashtags; post 3x/week; reply to every comment in the first hour
